## About me

I am a software developer, programming instructor, and technology professional focused on turning operational and educational challenges into maintainable digital solutions.

My work combines **full-stack development, applied artificial intelligence, workflow automation, technical training, and IT infrastructure**. I build web applications, management platforms, dashboards, public information portals, and educational tools using technologies such as React, Next.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, Python, Supabase, and PostgreSQL.

Alongside software development, I support an institutional environment with more than **300 Linux and Windows computers**, using SSH, Ansible, Bash, PowerShell, and systems administration tools. Teaching has always been part of my professional practice: I teach programming and robotics and have experience delivering technical instruction and professional training.

I also hold a master's degree in Geography and am pursuing a PhD focused on applied geospatial technologies, geomorphometry, photogrammetry, and spatial analysis.

- Based in Londrina, Brazil
- Open to remote roles worldwide and hybrid opportunities in Londrina
- Interested in full-time, contract, and freelance work
- English: B2 working proficiency; available for interviews and technical assessments
